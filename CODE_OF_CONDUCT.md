# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Synapse Pet welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Accept feedback and focus on the work rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal data, logs, screenshots, or credentials without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information or encouraging unwanted contact.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly ignoring reasonable moderation requests.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone represents the project. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in public issues.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
